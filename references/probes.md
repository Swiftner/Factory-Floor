# Probes — Challenging the Framing

**Mindset:** founders arrive with a diagnosis, not a symptom, and the diagnosis is
usually the thing they'd prefer to be true — it points at work they already know how
to do. Find the question they're avoiding and ask it once, plainly.

**Test their framing; don't dismiss it.** They have context you don't, and a founder
who is right and gets argued with stops telling you things. The probe is a question,
not a rebuttal — and this is JUDGMENT mode, so you're looking for the test, not the verdict.

Ask one. Wait. Don't stack probes.

---

## The question behind the question

**These are your internal read, not lines to say.** Several of them are claims about
the founder rather than the venture, and said out loud they land as criticism of the
person — which measurably degrades performance on novel work. Translate to the task
before you open your mouth.

| They ask | The real question |
|---|---|
| "Should we build X?" | "Would X bring customers who wouldn't otherwise arrive?" |
| "How do we grow faster?" | "Which funnel step is actually broken?" |
| "Should we raise?" | "Can we get to default alive without it?" |
| "What should we prioritize?" | "What's stopping customers succeeding right now?" |
| "Why is churn high?" | "Onboarded wrong, sold to the wrong people, or missed the job?" |
| "We're spread too thin" | "What shouldn't we be doing at all?" |
| "How do we close more deals?" | "Where exactly does the deal die?" |

## Nine usually-wrong diagnoses — and when they're right

Priors, not verdicts. Ask the probe, then take the answer seriously.

| Claim | Probe | It's right when |
|---|---|---|
| "We need more features" | "Do the customers you have stay?" | Churn concentrates on one capability they name unprompted, or you lose the same head-to-head deal repeatedly |
| "We need more marketing" | "Walk me through the first 10 minutes after signup." | Activation and retention are healthy and the funnel is simply thin |
| "We need to hire" | "What can't get done today that a hire fixes?" | Exploit and subordinate are exhausted and the bottleneck is hours, not focus |
| "Our pipeline is thin" | "Are the people who do reach out the right profile?" | Inbound is the right profile, volume is just low |
| "We're not converting demos" | "What do they say? Do you believe them?" | The same objection reproduces across independent buyers |
| "The market isn't ready" | "Who solves this now — even badly?" | No workaround exists, no budget line, trigger hasn't happened. Rare but real in regulated and pre-category markets |
| "We need better positioning" | "What two things do you want to own?" | They already hold two defended associations and the market moved |
| "We need to differentiate" | "Logo removed — could anyone tell it's you?" | Distinctive assets are strong and they're losing on meaning, not recognition |
| "Everyone is a customer" | "One customer type for six months — who?" | Almost never. Horizontal tools still pick one segment to start |

When the evidence supports them, **say so and move to how, not whether.** Contrarian
by reflex is exactly as useless as agreeable by reflex. The tell that you're being
reflexive: you reached for the probe before you heard their number.

## By situation

**Doesn't know what to work on** — "What single metric, if it doubled, would make
everything else easier?" Then: "Why aren't you working on that?"

**Deal stalled** — "Walk me through the last exchange. Where did it go quiet?" Never
accept "they went dark."

**Churn is high** — "When do people leave — day 1, 30, or 90?" The timing names it:
activation, value delivery, or wrong-fit.

**Busy but nothing ships** — "List everything in progress." Count it. More than 3 per
person is the answer.

**Pre-revenue and stuck** — "Have you asked anyone to pay — not in principle,
actually pay, now?"

**Wants to build before selling** — "What would you need to see to feel confident
selling this today?" It's almost always imaginary.

## Anti-patterns

| Pattern | Probe |
|---|---|
| Feature queue as therapy | "If you shipped it tomorrow, how many new customers?" |
| Only friendly users | "When did you last talk to someone who cancelled?" |
| Optimizing the wrong step | "Which step has the worst conversion right now?" |
| Consensus roadmap | "Who decides what the team works on? One name." |
| Vanity metrics as cover | "What metric would embarrass you in front of an investor?" |
| Hiring to solve a clarity problem | "What would they do in week one?" |
| "Looks cool" as validation | "How many said 'I'd pay right now'? Not try — pay." |
| Pitching instead of listening | "Last customer call — what percentage was them talking?" |
