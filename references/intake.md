# Intake — First Conversation

**Mindset:** you're establishing facts, not building rapport. Founders answer in
adjectives; you need integers. Every vague answer you accept becomes a wrong
diagnosis later.

**One question at a time. Never fire a list.**

---

## Five things to establish

1. **"Do you have paying customers yet — even one?"**
   No → pre-revenue. Yes → how many, how long. **"We had some but not anymore"** →
   restart.
2. **"How many people are on this full-time?"** 1–2 → focus or WIP. 3–10 →
   coordination, check for peanut-buttering. 10+ → policy constraints likely.
3. **"Roughly what's your MRR?"** <$10K → early growth. $10K–100K → usually
   activation or retention. $100K+ → scaling or coordination breakdown.
4. **"What's the one thing that, if it got better, would make everything else
   easier?"** Don't accept "everything." Push: *"If you had to pick one?"* Their
   answer tells you where they *think* it is — data about them, not about the
   constraint.
5. **"What have you already tried?"** Reveals the bias and stops you suggesting
   what they've ruled out.

## Vague answers

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "A few conversations" | "How many exactly? Name three." |
| "Pretty positive feedback" | "Did anyone say 'I'd pay right now'? Their words?" |
| "They were interested" | "Interested enough to do what?" |
| "We've validated the problem" | "How many paid before you built it? How much?" |
| "The feedback was good" | "What specifically? What was missing?" |
| "They went dark" | "Last thing you said? Last thing they said? Walk me through it." |

**"Looks cool" is not validation.** Only money, committed time, or an action that
cost them something.

## Then run the funnel break scan

Before routing to any stage file, once you know they have (or had) customers.
Protocol → `references/playbooks.md`. The stage gives context; the scan gives the
constraint.

## Enough to proceed

Paying customers or not · rough team size · where the funnel breaks · the symptom
they're worried about · what they've tried. Have all five? Skip to triage.

## Protocols they can name

*"Run the napkin test"* · *"Run the five tests"* · *"Run the Mafia Offer protocol"* ·
*"Run the funnel break scan"* · *"Do my weekly review"* · *"Run the positioning
sprint"* · *"Run a premortem"* — all in `references/playbooks.md`.
